The Top 5 Global Fruits

1 : Banana

A banana is an elongated, edible fruit that is botanically a berry[1]
produced by several kinds of large treelike herbaceous flowering plants
in the genus Musa. In some countries, cooking bananas are called plantains,
distinguishing them from dessert bananas. Read more or Explore

2 : Watermelons

The watermelon (Citrullus lanatus) is a species of flowering plant
in the family Cucurbitaceae. It is a scrambling and trailing vine-like
plant cultivated worldwide.Watermelons are grown in tropical to
temperate regions for its large edible berry having a firm rind and
no internal divisions; botanically, it is a pepo. Read more or Explore
